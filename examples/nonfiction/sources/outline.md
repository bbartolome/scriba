# Outline notes

Working title: Practice and Expertise.

The book asks why some people become very good at a skill and others plateau. It takes a practical view: expertise is built from deliberate practice, feedback and a long run of small corrections.

1. Cold open. A violinist, a surgeon and a chess player each describe the moment they first noticed they were improving.
2. The plateau. Why repeating a task does not make a person better, and what changes when the task is slightly beyond current ability.
3. The 10,000 hour idea. What the popular version gets right, what it gets wrong, and why hours alone do not explain skill.
4. Feedback loops. How coaches, tests and direct observation shorten the distance between an attempt and a correction.
5. Building a practice routine. Small sessions, clear goals and a record of errors.
6. Closing. Expertise as a habit of looking for the next error.
