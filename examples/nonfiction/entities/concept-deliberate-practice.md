name: Deliberate practice
definition: Effortful work aimed at one specific weakness, with feedback that arrives quickly.
---

# Examples and where it is used

The practice notes set deliberate practice apart from ordinary repetition by three features: a narrow goal, an attempt slightly beyond current ability, and quick feedback [@practice-notes]. Their example is a pianist who works on one passage, not the whole concerto [@practice-notes]. The outline's introduction names deliberate practice as one of the sources of expertise, with feedback and small corrections [@outline].
